## 1. What Premium includes

Premium unlocks the full course library, premium learning paths, additional
interactive practice, expanded audio access and advanced progress insights.
The exact benefits shown in the app at the time of purchase apply.

Free content remains available without a subscription. We do not require a
purchase to complete the core learning journey.

## 2. Plans and prices

SmartMe Premium is offered as a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription.
The price, billing period and any introductory offer are shown in the app and in
the Google Play purchase sheet before you confirm.

- Prices are displayed in Indian rupees (INR) and include applicable taxes where
  required.
- The backend plan catalogue and Google Play Console must use the same product
  IDs and base plans.
- If a price shown in the app differs from the Google Play purchase sheet, the
  Google Play sheet controls the transaction.
- We may test prices with real users. A price test never changes the amount you
  have already agreed to pay for an active period.

We do not currently offer a free trial. If a trial is introduced, its length,
conversion price and cancellation deadline will be disclosed before you start.

---

## 3. Billing and auto-renewal

- Payment is charged to your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase.
- The subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period unless
  you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date, or as required by Google
  Play's current rules.
- Google Play may retry a failed payment. We may grant a short grace period
  (currently 3 days) while the store retries, after which Premium access ends.
- The renewal charge is the price in effect for your plan, as shown by Google
  Play. We will give notice of a price increase where required by law or store
  policy.

We do not store your card, UPI or bank credentials. Google Play processes the
payment.

## 4. Managing and cancelling

You can manage or cancel in Google Play:

1. Open the Google Play Store.
2. Tap your profile → **Payments & subscriptions** → **Subscriptions**.
3. Select SmartMe and choose **Cancel subscription**.

You can also use the **Manage in Play** action on the SmartMe Premium screen.
Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep Premium until the end of the period
you have already paid for, unless the store or law requires otherwise.

Deleting the SmartMe app or your SmartMe account does not cancel a Google Play
subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first.

---

## 5. Refunds

Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy and applicable consumer law.
We do not control the store's payment system, but we will help with a genuine
billing problem.

To request help:

1. Check the purchase in Google Play's subscription centre.
2. Contact Google Play support for a store refund.
3. If the issue is with SmartMe access or verification, email
   support@smartme.app with the order ID (do not send your full card details).

We may issue a refund or credit where required by law, where you were charged in
error, or where a verified technical fault prevented access. Approving a refund
may end the associated entitlement.

---

## 6. Restoring a purchase

If you reinstall the app or sign in on another device, use **Restore purchase**
on the Premium screen. The backend verifies the purchase with Google Play before
granting access.

If restore fails:

- Confirm you are signed in with the Google account used for the purchase.
- Confirm the subscription is active in Google Play.
- Check your network connection.
- Contact support@smartme.app with the order ID and the approximate purchase
  date. Never share your Google password.

---

## 7. Entitlement and verification

Premium access is server-authoritative:

- The app opens the Google Play purchase flow.
- The backend verifies the purchase token with Google Play.
- Access is granted only after successful verification.
- Refunds, chargebacks, cancellations and expired subscriptions remove access
  after the applicable grace period.
- We may reconcile entitlements periodically and revoke access that the store
  reports as invalid.

If you believe a valid subscription was removed in error, contact support with
the order ID.

an appropriate remedy. Material changes will be communicated in the app or by
email where required.

If we discontinue Premium entirely, we will provide the notice and remedy
required by applicable law and store policy.
